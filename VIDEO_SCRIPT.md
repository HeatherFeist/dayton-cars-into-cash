# Home-page video — script

Optional. The sample site plays `public/explainer.mp4`. That file belongs to the sample yard. Replace it with your own clip, or clear `videoPath` in `site.config.js` to hide the video.

Read the business name, city, and phone number out of `site.config.js` while you record. Do not leave the sample city's name in the video.

About 40 seconds. Friendly, local, no pressure.

> Hi. Got an old car taking up space? Here in [your city], we turn junk cars into cash.
>
> It's three easy steps.
>
> One: tell us the year, make, model, and condition. It takes about a minute.
>
> Two: we call you with a cash offer. No obligation.
>
> Three: we come pick it up. If free towing is something you really offer, say so here. You get paid when we pick the vehicle up — say "cash or a check" only if that is how you pay.
>
> Call [your phone number], or send the form on this page. Let's turn that car into cash.

Only say "free towing", "same day", or "no title" if those lines are turned on in `site.config.js` and they are true for your yard.
